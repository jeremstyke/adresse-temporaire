Temporary Email With Password: Keep the Same Address

Published November 21, 2026, 5 min read

A temporary email with a password is what most people look for after their first bad surprise: they signed up somewhere with a throwaway address, came back two days later, and the inbox was gone. What you really want is not a password as such, it is a disposable address that stays yours, that nobody else can open, and that you can find again.

Why does a normal temp mail address disappear?

A basic temp mail address has no owner. The site gives you a random name, keeps it in your browser, and erases messages after a short time (one hour on this site). If you clear your browser data, switch phones or simply forget the name, there is nothing to log back into.

The second issue is privacy. With no owner, the inbox is public: anyone who types the exact address can read it. For a one-time download code, that is fine. For an account you plan to keep using, it is not.

How do I keep the same temporary address?

You reserve it. On Jetbox Mail, a free account lets you claim one address so that it belongs to you. From then on:

  1. Only you can read its messages, from any device where you are signed in.
  2. Messages are kept 24 hours instead of one hour.
  3. You can turn on a Telegram alert for each new message.

To do it, open My account, enter your usual email, type the 6-digit code you receive, and reserve the address shown on screen or pick a name of your own.

Is there really no password?

Right, there is no password to create or forget. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your real email. That is the "password" part handled for you, without one more string of characters to store somewhere.

It also means your real address is only used to let you in. Websites you sign up to with the temporary address never see it.

How long does a reserved address last?

On the free plan, your reserved address stays yours as long as it keeps receiving mail. If it gets no message at all for 30 days, it is released so that names do not pile up unused. Premium addresses do not have that limit while your plan is active.

So a reserved temp address is a good fit for a trial that lasts a few weeks, a forum you visit now and then, or a newsletter you want to read without filling your main inbox. It is still not the place for your bank.

Can I choose the name of my address?

Yes. Instead of the random name, tap Pick my address and type something like shop.tests. A few tips:

  • Avoid your real name or anything that points to you, or the address stops being anonymous.
  • Short, common words are often taken already. Add a word or a number.
  • The same name works on both domains, @jetboxmail.com and @adresse-temporaire.fr, and lands in the same inbox.

What if a website refuses the address?

Some sites block known temp mail domains when you sign up. Before giving up, switch domains: your reserved name works on both @jetboxmail.com and @adresse-temporaire.fr, and a site that blocks one may accept the other. Messages sent to either one arrive in the same private inbox, so you do not have to manage two accounts.

If both are refused, the site has decided it only wants long-term addresses. That is its right. You can then either skip the service or give it an address you are happy to keep, ideally one you use only for signups so your personal inbox stays quiet. Trying to sneak past the block with tricks is rarely worth the time, and it can get the new account closed later.

When is a reserved temp address still a bad idea?

Ask yourself one question: if this account got locked tomorrow, would I need to recover it? If the answer is yes, use a long-term address you control. A temporary inbox, even a private one, is built to be let go. Accounts that hold money, medical data, official documents or your work belong on an address you will keep for years.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get my old temp mail address back without an account?

Only if it is still saved in the same browser. Without an account the address is not linked to you, so there is no way to prove it is yours. Reserve it next time.

Do I need Premium to keep my address?

No. The free account already gives you one private address. Premium is for people who want several addresses, 30 days of storage, attachments or forwarding.

Can I use the reserved address on my phone too?

Yes. Sign in with the same email on your phone and your addresses are there. You can also link the Telegram bot to get your codes inside Telegram.
