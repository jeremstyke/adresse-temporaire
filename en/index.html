    Why use a temporary email address?

    Every time you give out your real address, you risk years of marketing emails, or seeing it leak in a data breach. A disposable address protects you: try a service, download a white paper or get on a Wi-Fi network without leaving anything behind.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes, completely, with no sign-up.

    How long are messages kept?

    One hour after they arrive, then they are permanently deleted. Nothing is kept.

    Can I send emails?

    No. The address can only receive, so it can't be used to send spam.

    Can someone else read my messages?

    Anyone who knows the exact address can see its inbox. Don't use it for anything sensitive (banking, health, government) and prefer a randomly generated address.

    Will I get my address back if I come back?

    Yes, it's kept in your browser. You can also create a new one at any time.

    Why do some sites reject the address?

    Some services block disposable addresses. In that case, use your usual address.
